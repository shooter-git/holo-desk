# Code of conduct

HO[LO] Desk is made by people who love music, for people who love music. Everyone who takes part (in
issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives) is expected to:

- be kind and patient, and assume good intent;
- give and take criticism of work, never of people;
- welcome newcomers and questions of every level;
- respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sharing others' private information, and sexualised or
violent content are not tolerated.

If something happens that makes you uncomfortable, report it privately to the maintainers through
GitHub's [private reporting](https://github.com/shooter-git/holo-desk/security/advisories/new) (choose
"conduct" in your message). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content and
restrict or ban anyone who breaks this code.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
